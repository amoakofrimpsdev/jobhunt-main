JOBHUNT FOR WINDOWS
===================

Jobhunt finds jobs straight from employers' own job boards, ranks them against your
profile, and tracks your applications. Everything stays on your computer.

START
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (for example Documents\Jobhunt). Keep Jobhunt.exe
   and the "app" folder together.
2. Double-click Jobhunt.exe.
   - Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not signed:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - If Windows Defender Firewall asks about Jobhunt: click Allow (private networks)
     if you want to use Jobhunt on your phone, otherwise Cancel.
3. Jobhunt opens in your web browser. Keep the small black window open while you use
   it; close that window to quit.

FIRST STEPS
- Profile: add the job titles you want and your skills (or upload your resume), set
  Countries (United States, United Kingdom, both, anywhere) and whether you need visa
  sponsorship. Save.
- Jobs: the first visit reads the job boards. Jobs appear within minutes and keep
  arriving for a while. Open a job to see why it matches and to apply on the
  employer's own site.
- Tracker: mark jobs Saved, Applied, Interviewing and so on.
- Alerts: on the Jobs page, "Save as alert" keeps a search and collects new matches.
  New matches also pop up as a Windows notification (from "Windows PowerShell").

ON YOUR PHONE (optional)
In Jobhunt's Settings, under Phone, switch on phone access and scan the code with your
phone's camera (same Wi-Fi), or
open the address shown there in the phone's browser and type the six-letter code. To keep it like an app: iPhone, Share > Add to Home Screen;
Android, menu > Add to Home screen. This computer has to stay on with Jobhunt open.

CHROME EXTENSION (optional, fills application forms)
1. Unzip Jobhunt-extension.zip into its own folder.
2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click "Load unpacked"
   and pick that folder.
3. In Jobhunt open Settings, copy the pairing code, click the Jobhunt icon in Chrome
   and paste it.
It fills forms but never presses Submit.

AI (optional)
Settings lets Jobhunt write cover letters and tailor resumes with a model on your own
computer (Ollama, LM Studio), your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, or Claude Desktop.
Keys are encrypted with your Windows sign-in: another account, or a copy of the file
on another computer, cannot read them.

YOUR DATA
Everything is in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Jobhunt (paste that into the File Explorer address bar).
Delete that folder to start over.
